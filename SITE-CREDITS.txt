jocoly.com landing scene asset sources

Rubber duck base mesh: Ryan King Art, Rubber Duck (CC0)
https://ryankingart.gumroad.com/l/duck
Modified in Blender for the interactive duck scene.

Earth surface: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, Blue Marble
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/3615

Moon surface: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720

International Space Station 3D model: NASA/Ames Research Center
https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/international-space-station-iss-a/
Converted from NASA's GLB to an uncompressed browser GLB with Blender.

The Sun texture, orbit geometry, shader effects, and space background are generated for this site.
